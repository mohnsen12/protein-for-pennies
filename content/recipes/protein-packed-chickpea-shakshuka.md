This protein-packed chickpea shakshuka takes a classic Middle Eastern favorite and turns it into a budget-friendly powerhouse. By simmering affordable pantry staples like crushed tomatoes and canned chickpeas with warm spices, you create a deeply flavorful sauce that serves as the perfect bed for perfectly poached eggs. It is a hearty, one-pan meal that comes together in under thirty minutes, making it an absolute lifesaver on busy weeknights.

Adding chickpeas not only stretches the recipe to feed a hungry family for pennies, but it also provides a substantial boost of plant-based protein and fiber. The smoked paprika adds a wonderful depth that tastes like it has been cooking all day, while the runny egg yolks create a rich, creamy sauce when broken. Grab a slice of crusty bread or pita to scoop up every last bite of this deeply comforting, incredibly nutritious dinner.
