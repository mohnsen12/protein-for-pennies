Swapping heavy cream for blended cottage cheese slashes the grocery bill while drastically boosting the protein content of this classic Italian-style dinner. Chicken thighs offer a richer flavor at a fraction of the cost of chicken breasts, making this dish both affordable and satisfying.

Blended cottage cheese creates a silky, luxurious sauce that coats every piece of pasta without feeling heavy. Garlic, Italian herbs, and juicy tomatoes give it classic Tuscan comfort food flavor in under 30 minutes.
