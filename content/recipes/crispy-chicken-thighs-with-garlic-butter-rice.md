Using bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs is one of the smartest ways to keep grocery bills low without sacrificing protein. This recipe maximizes value by cooking the rice directly in the rendered chicken fat, eliminating the need for expensive cooking oils. It is a complete, satisfying meal built entirely from pantry staples and affordable poultry cuts.

The magic happens in a single skillet where the chicken skin gets incredibly crispy while the rice absorbs all the savory garlic butter juices. It requires minimal cleanup and delivers rich, comforting flavors that make cheap ingredients taste like a luxury. It is the ultimate fuss-free weeknight dinner for anyone cooking on a budget.
