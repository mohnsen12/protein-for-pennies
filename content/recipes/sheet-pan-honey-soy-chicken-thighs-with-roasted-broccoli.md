Dark meat chicken thighs deliver rich moisture and generous protein at a fraction of the price of breasts. Paired with wallet-friendly frozen vegetables and bulk white rice, this sheet-pan meal keeps grocery bills firmly under two dollars a plate.

A fast five-ingredient pantry glaze turns glossy and sticky in a hot oven, caramelizing around tender chicken edges while crisping up the broccoli florets. Everything cooks side-by-side on a single pan, eliminating mountain-high sink piles on busy weeknights.
