Smoked sausage is one of the most reliable budget proteins in the supermarket, offering long refrigeration shelf life and intense savory flavor at a low price per pound. Combined with humble green cabbage and sturdy potatoes, this sheet-pan meal turns three inexpensive ingredients into a filling, nutrient-dense dinner with minimal expense.

High-heat oven roasting coaxes deep sweetness out of chopped cabbage while browning the sliced sausage and crisping the potatoes. Rendered fat from the kielbasa naturally seasons the vegetables as they cook together, resulting in big flavor with only one sheet pan to wash.
