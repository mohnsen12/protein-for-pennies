Chicken drumsticks remain the most reliable bargain cut in the poultry aisle, delivering rich flavor and high protein at a fraction of the cost of breasts. Pairing them with russet potatoes rounds out a hearty family dinner using standard pantry seasonings.

Lemon juice and dried herbs cut through the richness of dark meat while infusing the roasting potatoes with bright, savory pan drippings. One sheet pan handles the entire cooking process, keeping post-dinner cleanup fast and effortless.
