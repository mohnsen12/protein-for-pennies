Heavy cream and specialty sun-dried tomatoes drive traditional Tuscan chicken prices far beyond an everyday dinner budget. Swapping in blended cottage cheese mimics that rich, garlicky cream sauce at a fraction of the dairy case cost while nearly doubling the total protein. Built around economical chicken thighs and canned tomatoes, this weeknight skillet keeps the ingredient bill lean without losing comfort.

Pureeing the cottage cheese creates a velvety emulsion that blankets penne pasta and juicy browned chicken thigh pieces seamlessly. Earthy spinach and bright canned tomatoes balance the savory garlic notes, delivering restaurant depth straight from standard pantry staples. The entire meal comes together in thirty minutes with minimal dishes and reliable weeknight speed.
