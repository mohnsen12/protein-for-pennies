Swapping ground beef for dry brown lentils is one of the easiest ways to slash your grocery bill without sacrificing the hearty satisfaction of a classic pasta night. Dry lentils cost pennies per serving and expand beautifully as they absorb the savory tomato broth. This recipe delivers a thick, protein-rich sauce that feeds a family of four for less than the price of a single pound of beef.

By mimicking the traditional soffritto base of onions and carrots, and boosting the umami with a splash of soy sauce and beef bouillon, this sauce achieves an incredible depth of flavor. It simmers on the stovetop in just 30 minutes, making it a highly practical, low-effort weeknight dinner. Leftovers taste even better the next day as the flavors continue to meld.
