Sweet potatoes cost mere pennies per pound and provide a dense, slow-burning carbohydrate base that keeps dinner naturally filling without expensive specialty grains. Pairing them with budget-friendly chicken thighs keeps the protein high while staying well below the grocery threshold of chicken breast. A speedy microwave technique cuts total cooking time in half so you can get a hearty hot meal on the table in under thirty minutes.

The fiery tang of classic cayenne buffalo sauce cuts sharply through the natural sweetness of the roasted potato interior. Topped with a drizzle of cheap supermarket ranch, every forkful balances rich heat, cooling herb dressing, and tender shredded meat. It turns everyday pantry staples into a satisfying meal that feels like pub comfort food on a shoestring budget.
