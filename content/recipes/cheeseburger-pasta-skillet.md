This cheeseburger pasta skillet takes all the nostalgic flavors of a classic American diner burger and transforms them into an incredibly cheap, one-pan dinner. By using affordable ground beef and pantry staples like elbow macaroni and mustard, it builds a massive amount of comforting flavor without breaking the grocery budget. It is the perfect solution for feeding a hungry family fast.

Cooking the pasta directly in the seasoned beef broth means you save time on cleanup and the noodles absorb savory notes from the inside out. Once finished with a handful of sharp cheddar and a scattering of chopped dill pickles, it tastes shockingly close to a fast-food favorite. It is a practical, deeply satisfying meal that relies entirely on inexpensive ingredients you likely already own.
