Boneless chicken thighs are one of the most budget-friendly, high-protein meats available in the store while remaining incredibly juicy. Pairing them with low-cost staples like sliced potatoes and frozen sweet corn keeps this hearty dinner well under three dollars per serving.

Cooking everything together inside sealed foil pouches locks in humidity and allows the barbecue sauce and spices to infuse the vegetables. Best of all, dinner cooks entirely in its own pocket, leaving no greasy baking pans to scrub when you finish eating.
