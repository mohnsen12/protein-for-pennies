Ground chicken thighs deliver deep savory flavor and natural juiciness without the inflated cost of lean poultry cuts. Pairing substantial meatballs with pantry-staple cornmeal creates a heavy-hitting protein dinner for well under two dollars a plate. A single bag of standard cornmeal costs pennies per portion and stretches the meal into restaurant-level comfort.

Searing the meatballs develops deep caramelized notes before they gently braise in sweet tomato marinara, infusing the sauce with meat drippings. Simmering the cornmeal with milk and a touch of butter turns an humble grain into a luscious, velvety bed that catches every drop of sauce. The entire dinner comes together using common equipment in forty minutes flat.
