Using a simple combination of breadcrumbs and milk stretches one pound of ground beef into sixteen filling meatballs without sacrificing flavor or protein. Cooking dry orzo straight in tomato broth reduces dishwashing and ensures every grain absorbs maximum savory taste.

This entire meal comes together in forty minutes using inexpensive pantry staples. Seared beef, tender pasta, and simmered tomatoes deliver hearty comfort on a tight weeknight budget.
