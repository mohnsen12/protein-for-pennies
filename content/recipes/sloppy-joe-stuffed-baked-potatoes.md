Swapping out traditional hamburger buns for hearty russet potatoes is an easy way to stretch a single pound of ground beef into a filling, four-person dinner. Potatoes offer a cheaper, more nutrient-dense base than store-bought bread, keeping the grocery bill low. This recipe relies on basic pantry condiments to create a rich, savory sauce without any expensive pre-made mixes.

The contrast between the earthy, fluffy potato and the sweet, tangy ground beef filling creates a satisfying texture in every bite. It delivers the nostalgic taste of classic sloppy joes with minimal cleanup and zero fuss. Everyone will love the melty cheese finish on top of this comforting weeknight staple.
