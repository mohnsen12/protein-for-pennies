Green cabbage is one of the most budget-friendly vegetables in the produce aisle, delivering volume and crunch for pennies per serving. Pairing rich chicken thighs with hearty cabbage stretches a single pound of meat into four full meals without sacrificing protein. This recipe maximizes flavor while keeping grocery costs exceptionally low.

High-heat searing gives the cabbage sweet charred edges while keeping its satisfying bite. The quick soy, ginger, and garlic glaze coats every piece in a deeply savory, aromatic sauce. Best of all, the entire skillet comes together in just twenty minutes on a busy weeknight.
