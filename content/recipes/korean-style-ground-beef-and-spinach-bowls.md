Ground beef is one of the most versatile budget proteins available, and this recipe stretches it beautifully by pairing it with affordable pantry staples. Using standard white rice and a quick homemade sweet-savory sauce keeps the grocery bill low while maximizing satisfaction. It is a reliable, wallet-friendly alternative to expensive takeout.

The sauce relies on brown sugar, soy sauce, and garlic to mimic classic bulgogi flavors without requiring specialty marinades. Wilted spinach adds color and nutrients directly into the pan, and topping each bowl with a crispy fried egg boosts the protein and creates a rich, velvety sauce when broken.
