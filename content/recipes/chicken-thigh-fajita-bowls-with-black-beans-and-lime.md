Chicken thigh fajita bowls are a powerhouse meal when it comes to keeping grocery costs down while delivering huge protein numbers. By tossing the chicken directly in a dry spice rub, you skip the expensive liquid marinades entirely while still guaranteeing deeply seasoned, tender meat. Pairing affordable poultry with canned black beans stretches the filling across four hearty servings, keeping the overall price per bowl astonishingly low.

This recipe builds everything in one hot skillet, bringing the sizzling excitement of restaurant fajitas straight to your weeknight dinner table. Served over an inexpensive base of filling white rice and finished with a sharp squeeze of fresh lime, it cuts through the richness and brightens the entire dish. It is a vibrant, filling, and incredibly cheap dinner that feels anything but a compromise.
