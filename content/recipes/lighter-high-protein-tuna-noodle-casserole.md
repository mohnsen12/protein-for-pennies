Skipping expensive canned cream soups in favor of blended cottage cheese slashes sodium while dramatically boosting protein. Pantry staples like canned tuna and egg noodles keep total grocery costs well under two dollars per plate. It is a budget-friendly weeknight bake that transforms simple shelf-stable ingredients into a satisfying dinner.

Blending cottage cheese with a touch of milk creates a surprisingly rich, velvety sauce that mimics traditional baked casseroles without feeling heavy. Sweet frozen peas, savory tuna, and a sharp cheddar crisp topping deliver nostalgic comfort with modern nutrition.
