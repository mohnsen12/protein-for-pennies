Red lentils are one of the most budget-friendly protein sources available, cooking in under 20 minutes without any pre-soaking required. Combining them with staple white rice creates a complete protein profile that keeps total costs under $1.20 per serving. This simple skillet meal stretches basic spice rack staples into a deeply satisfying dinner.

A warm blend of curry powder, ginger, and simmered tomatoes gives this creamy dal incredible depth without needing expensive specialty ingredients. It comes together in a single skillet while the rice simmers, making cleanup fast and straightforward. Leftovers reheat exceptionally well, allowing flavors to deepen even further for next-day meals.
