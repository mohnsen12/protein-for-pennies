Replacing half of the traditional ground beef with high-fiber canned kidney beans slashes the meal cost by nearly half without dropping protein levels. Using basic pantry staples keeps total grocery expenditures low while yielding four heavy portions.

Rich chili spices and savory meat combine under a fluffy, sweet corn crust cooked in one single skillet. It cooks in just 35 minutes, creating a mess-free weeknight dinner that balances sweet and spicy flavors perfectly.
