Using bone-in chicken thighs is the ultimate budget hack for creating a rich, deeply flavorful soup base without buying expensive stock. Searing the thighs first renders out savory fat to cook the vegetables, keeping ingredient costs incredibly low. This recipe stretches a single package of chicken into six filling portions by bulk-loading with inexpensive carrots and long-grain rice.

The carrots lend a natural sweetness that balances the savory, garlic-infused broth, while the rice absorbs the rich chicken flavor as it cooks directly in the pot. It is a simple, one-pot cleanup meal that delivers classic comfort food on a shoestring budget. Perfect for chilly weeknights or meal prep containers for the week ahead.
